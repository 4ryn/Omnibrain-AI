TITLE: Product documentation for Support
SOURCE: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/9196895052442-Product-documentation-for-Support
SOURCE: Official Zendesk Help

The comprehensive Support documentation covers Zendesk basics, Agent Workspace, security and data protection, ticket management, ticket customization, ticket automation and collaboration, measuring success, and multiple language support.

Ticket channels include email, voice, messaging, live chat, and Web Widget.

AI and automation topics include AI agents, Copilot, omnichannel routing, objects and rules.

Account and user topics include account management, managing people, workforce management and QA, user access, and user sign-in.

Self-service and knowledge, apps and integrations, developer documentation, and analytics through Explore are also covered.
