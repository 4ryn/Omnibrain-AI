TITLE: About Zendesk triggers and how they work
SOURCE: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408822236058-About-Zendesk-triggers-and-how-they-work
SOURCE: Official Zendesk Help

Triggers fire only when their configured conditions are met. Triggers contain two parts: conditions and actions. They can be understood as “if” and “then” statements: if a record contains a specified set of conditions, the actions update the record and can send notifications.

Condition statements are the “if” part of a trigger and are structured as a condition/category, an operator, and a value. Available condition options vary by trigger type.

Triggers, like other business rules, must be smaller than 65 KB.
