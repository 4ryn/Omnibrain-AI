TITLE: About automations and how they work
SOURCE: https://support.zendesk.com/hc/en-us/articles/4408832701850-About-automations-and-how-they-work
SOURCE: Official Zendesk Help

Automations manage tickets by executing actions based on time events. They run hourly on non-closed tickets when conditions are met.

Automations are similar to triggers because both use conditions and actions. They differ because automations act when a time-based event occurs after a ticket property was set or updated, rather than immediately after a ticket is created or updated.

Important facts include:
- Automations run every hour, but not necessarily at the top of the hour.
- An automation can fire on a maximum of 1,000 tickets per hour.
- Automations do not run on closed tickets.
- Each ticket can be updated a maximum of 100 times by automations.
- An automation must include a condition that is true only once or an action that nullifies at least one condition.
- Up to 500 active automations can exist.
